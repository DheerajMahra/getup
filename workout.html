Gym Week

Five morning gym sessions, two evening runs, Tuesday and Thursday free. Built for muscle gain with minimal fat gain, with every major muscle group trained about twice a week.

Shoulders + traps + light back

Primary shoulder dayEvening rest
  • Shoulders
  • Traps

The chest-supported row keeps extra load off the lower back after Wednesday's deadlifts. Friday's chest pressing also trains the front delts, so keep the overhead press controlled if your shoulders haven't recovered.

No conventional or Romanian deadlift on this day.
